FinPrep — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

FinPrep does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. We operate no servers that receive your information.

Where your data lives

All financial information you enter (income, expenses, debts, net worth, goals) is stored only on your device, encrypted at rest (AES-GCM with a key held in the device Keychain) and protected by iOS file protection. You may optionally require Face ID / Touch ID to open the app.

Network use

The only network request the app can make is an optional check for an updated public rate dataset (pay/allowance/tax tables). It sends no personal data — only downloads a public file — and the app works fully offline.

Data sharing

We do not share data with third parties. Files you choose to export (PDF, Excel, CSV) are handled entirely by you through the iOS share sheet; we never receive them.

Purchases

FinPrep Pro is processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Your control

You can edit or delete your data anytime, or remove all of it by deleting the app.
